NUTRITION MONTH 2026

Eat Healthy, Stay Healthy!

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July to remind everyone to eat healthy foods and live a healthy lifestyle. Good nutrition helps us grow strong, stay active, and prevent diseases.

Fruits

Fruits are rich in vitamins and minerals. Examples are apples, bananas, oranges, grapes, and mangoes.

Vegetables

Vegetables help keep our bodies healthy. Eat green leafy vegetables every day.

Healthy Drinks

Drink plenty of water every day. Water keeps our body hydrated and healthy.

Healthy Plate

A healthy plate should include different kinds of nutritious foods. Half of your plate should have fruits and vegetables. The other half should have rice or whole grains and protein like fish, chicken, eggs, or beans.

Eating a balanced meal every day helps you stay healthy, strong, and active.

Exercise

Exercise keeps our heart and body strong. Walk, run, dance, or play sports every day.

Foods to Avoid

Avoid eating too much junk food, candy, and soft drinks. Choose healthy foods instead.

Benefits of Healthy Eating

• Gives us energy.

• Helps us grow strong.

• Prevents diseases.

• Improves concentration.

• Keeps us healthy.

Healthy Family

Eat healthy meals, drink water, exercise, and get enough sleep every day.

Healthy Food = Healthy Life
